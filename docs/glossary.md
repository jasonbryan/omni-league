# Glossary

The shared vocabulary for Omni League: the product's domain terms and the terms of the development process. It is an index, not a second copy of the rules. Each entry is one or two sentences and links the document that defines it.

- **Use these terms exactly** in code, issues, PRs, and UI copy. Do not coin a synonym for a term listed here.
- **If a needed term is missing,** add it here in the same PR that first uses it.
- **The linked documents win on conflict.** If an entry disagrees with its source, the source is right and the entry is fixed.

Entries marked **Working definition — not yet specified by the owner** or **Working assumption** describe how a word is used today, not a decided requirement. Open questions about them are listed at the end; [docs/product/requirements.md](product/requirements.md) remains the source of truth for product behaviour.

## Product (domain) terms

- **Account:** the sign-in identity of a user. Only adults hold accounts at launch, and they sign in with email + password or Google. An invite needs no account, but accepting one, joining a team, or registering does. Source: [requirements, Constraints](product/requirements.md#constraints), [requirements, Accounts](product/requirements.md#accounts-players-and-guardians), [ADR 0008](decisions/0008-auth-provider.md).
- **Account link:** the top bar's link to `/app` that replaces "Sign in" once the browser knows the user is signed in: initials and the user's name. Approved in `design/mockups/63-sign-in.html` (#63).
- **Admin area:** the signed-in pages at `/app/organizations/<slug>` where an organization's org admins manage it: Overview, Locations, Leagues, and Staff, in the app shell with an admin sidebar. Anyone else gets the no-access view. Proposed in `design/mockups/65-organization-admin-locations.html` (#65, #66, #67).
- **Adult confirmation:** the required "I am 18 or older" box on sign-up. The server refuses a sign-up without it and records it as `users.adultConfirmedAt`. Source: [ADR 0008](decisions/0008-auth-provider.md), #62.
- **Age group (division):** the last part of a **Division**: Adult (the default), an optional adult bracket (30+, 35+, 40+, 50+, 55+, 60+), or a youth group U6 to U19. A youth group reads gender as Boys or Girls. Keys such as `over40` and `u10` in `convex/lib/divisions.ts`.
- **Application fee:** the platform's cut of a payment to an organization, collected through Stripe Connect. It is the greater of a percentage of the payment and the per-player floor for the roster entries the payment pays for, so listing fewer players does not lower the percentage; in free seasons and for offline payments the platform bills the organization's connected account. On Stripe it is the `application_fee_amount` of a direct charge, or an invoice item on a platform bill. Source: [requirements, Payments](product/requirements.md#payments), [ADR 0012](decisions/0012-stripe-connect.md).
- **Captain:** the player who registers a team, invites people to it, and reports its scores. Source: [requirements, Registration](product/requirements.md#registration), [Schedule, scores, and standings](product/requirements.md#schedule-scores-and-standings).
- **Connected account:** an organization's own Stripe account, linked to the platform through Stripe Connect; players' payments go to it. It is a Stripe Accounts v2 account with the full Stripe Dashboard; Stripe collects its processing fees and carries its losses. Source: [requirements, Payments](product/requirements.md#payments), [ADR 0012](decisions/0012-stripe-connect.md).
- **Custom level:** An organization's own level label for one sport, such as Gold, mapped to a standard level so filters work across organizations; shown publicly as "Gold (A)" (`customLevelLabel` in `convex/lib/divisions.ts`). The league does not store it yet. Proposed in `design/mockups/300-public-site-league-form.html`.
- **Division:** A league's classification, set on the league: **Gender**, players per side (**Format**), **Level**, and **Age group**. Shown as "Coed · 4v4 · BB", with " · U10" or " · 40+" added when the age group is not Adult. The values, labels, and name-fill rules are in `convex/lib/divisions.ts`; the league does not store these yet. Proposed in `design/mockups/300-public-site-filters.html` and `300-public-site-league-form.html`.
- **Draft:** a league or season that an organizer has not yet published. Drafts stay off public pages; the organization's staff see them in the signed-in app. Source: [requirements, Organizations and public registration](product/requirements.md#organizations-and-public-registration).
- **Email verification:** confirming that a user owns the email address on a password account, by a link or code sent to it. Required before the first production release. Source: [requirements, Constraints](product/requirements.md#constraints), [ADR 0011](decisions/0011-transactional-email.md).
- **Expired invite:** a staff invite whose 14 days have passed. It keeps the status `pending`, stays on the Staff screen as "Expired <date>", and blocks a new invite to the same address until an org admin revokes it. Source: [#67](https://github.com/jasonbryan/omni-league/issues/67), `design/mockups/67-organization-staff.html` (decision 8).
- **Find a league page:** **Proposal, not yet chosen by the owner.** `/leagues`, the public search page the public top bar's "Find a league" opens: a GET form over published leagues by league, organization, or location, with sport chips, so every search has a URL. Proposed in `design/mockups/300-public-site-navigation.html`.
- **Format (sport format):** the way a sport is played, given as the number of players per side and written "4v4" (never "4 v 4"). A division offers a range per sport: beach volleyball 2 to 6, soccer 4 to 11, softball 9 to 10. A season's format sets its minimum roster size (beach volleyball 2, 4, or 6; soccer 5, 7, or 11; softball 10), which an organization may raise but not lower. Configured in `convex/lib/sports.ts` (season formats) and `convex/lib/divisions.ts` (division range). Source: [requirements, Payments](product/requirements.md#application-fee-cannot-be-avoided).
- **Free agent:** an individual who registers for a season without a team; an organizer places them on a team. A rostered player cannot also be a free agent in the same season. Source: [requirements, Registration](product/requirements.md#registration), [Seasons](product/requirements.md#seasons).
- **Game:** **Working definition — not yet specified by the owner.** A single scheduled contest between two teams in a season, with a time and location; captains report its score. Source: [requirements, Schedule, scores, and standings](product/requirements.md#schedule-scores-and-standings).
- **Gender (division):** the first part of a **Division**: Coed, Men's, or Women's; with a youth **Age group**, Coed, Boys, or Girls. There is no "Open" gender. Keys `coed`, `mens`, `womens` in `convex/lib/divisions.ts`.
- **Get set up:** the checklist on the admin Overview that links an organization's first steps to their pages: add a location, create a league, publish it, and invite staff (optional). It shows until the organization has a published league. #65 ships its first step, #66 adds the league steps, and #67 the staff step. Proposed in `design/mockups/65-organization-admin-locations.html` (#65).
- **Guardian:** an adult account holder who adds and manages a minor's roster entry and may also play (owner, 2026-10-10). The guardian adds the minor when accepting a team invite; the entry holds only the minor's display name and links to the guardian's user id. Source: [requirements, Accounts](product/requirements.md#accounts-players-and-guardians), [ADR 0008](decisions/0008-auth-provider.md).
- **Install (installed app):** adding the app to a device's home screen, after which it opens in its own window with its own icon and no address bar. Browsers offer it from the web app manifest; on iPhone and iPad it is Safari's Share, then Add to Home Screen, and push notifications work there only once installed. Source: [requirements, Installable app (PWA)](product/requirements.md#installable-app-pwa).
- **Invite:** a request, sent by a captain or organizer to an email address, to join a team or the organization's staff. The invited person needs no account to receive it, but signs up or signs in to accept it. A pending invite is not a roster entry and carries no application fee. Source: [requirements, Accounts](product/requirements.md#accounts-players-and-guardians).
- **Invite link:** the `<origin>/invites/accept#<token>` link an org admin copies when creating a staff invite and sends themselves. It is shown once, because only the token's hash is stored; to resend, the org admin revokes the invite and creates a new one, unless the revoke is still inside the undo window. Proposed in `design/mockups/67-organization-staff.html` (#67, #70).
- **League:** a sports league that belongs to one organization, is held at its locations, and runs repeated seasons. It is a draft until an organizer publishes it; users register for its seasons from its public page. Source: [requirements](product/requirements.md#organizations-and-public-registration), [Seasons](product/requirements.md#seasons).
- **League management page:** the signed-in page at `/app/organizations/<org>/leagues/<league>` where an org admin, or a league organizer assigned to the league, lists the league's seasons, creates draft seasons, and publishes them. Proposed in `design/mockups/125-126-seasons.html` (#125, #126).
- **League organizer:** a staff role that runs the specific leagues an org admin assigns to it, within one organization. Source: [requirements, Organizations and staff](product/requirements.md#organizations-and-staff).
- **Level (division):** a division's skill tier, from the sport's standard ladder: beach volleyball Rec, B, BB, A, AA, Open; soccer and softball Rec, Intermediate, Competitive. An organization may use its own **Custom level** mapped to a standard one. The ladders are in `convex/lib/divisions.ts`.
- **Location:** a place where an organization's leagues are held. An organization can have several, and each has a public page. Source: [requirements](product/requirements.md#organizations-and-public-registration), [ADR 0006](decisions/0006-angular-app-baseline.md).
- **Maskable icon:** the app icon variant whose art keeps to a central safe zone (a circle of 80% of the width), so an Android launcher can crop it to any shape. Approved in `design/mockups/80-app-icon.html` and shipped as `public/icons/icon-maskable-512.png` (#80).
- **Maximum roster size:** the most roster entries a team in a season may have, set by an organizer at or above the season's minimum roster size. Source: [requirements, Seasons](product/requirements.md#seasons).
- **Maximum teams:** the most teams a season takes. A season that has reached it is **full**, and new teams go on its waitlist. Source: [requirements, Seasons](product/requirements.md#seasons).
- **Membership:** a user's role in one organization: `orgAdmin` or `leagueOrganizer` (with the leagues assigned to them). A user has at most one per organization; an organization's creator gets `orgAdmin`. Source: `convex/schema.ts`, `convex/lib/access.ts`, #64.
- **Minimum roster size:** the fewest roster entries a team needs before it is confirmed, set per sport format in the sport configuration (beach volleyball 2, 4, or 6; soccer 5, 7, or 11; softball 10); an organization may raise it but not lower it. Part of the application-fee mechanism. Source: [requirements, Payments](product/requirements.md#payments).
- **Minor:** a person below adult age. Minors hold no account at launch and appear only as roster entries managed by a guardian or organizer; only a display name is stored, and opponents see the first name and last initial. Source: [requirements, Accounts](product/requirements.md#accounts-players-and-guardians), [requirements, Constraints](product/requirements.md#constraints), [ADR 0008](decisions/0008-auth-provider.md).
- **New-version prompt:** the card at the bottom of the window that says a new version of the app is ready, with Reload and Later (Later hides it until the app is next opened); the service worker downloaded the version in the background. Its other state, the **unrecoverable message**, asks the user to reload when the worker lost files it needs. See `design/mockups/81-update-prompt.html` and [ADR 0013](decisions/0013-pwa-stack.md) section 4 (#81).
- **No-access view:** what the admin area shows a signed-in league organizer or non-member: a message that only org admins can open it, and a link to Your organizations. No organization data loads. Proposed in `design/mockups/65-organization-admin-locations.html` (#65).
- **Org admin:** a staff role that manages everything in one organization and invites people. One person can hold roles in several organizations. Source: [requirements, Organizations and staff](product/requirements.md#organizations-and-staff).
- **Offline note:** the message the app shows while the device has no usable connection, saying the user is offline and that changes need a connection. Source: [requirements, Installable app (PWA)](product/requirements.md#installable-app-pwa).
- **Offline payment:** a team or roster entry an organizer marks as paid outside the platform. The application fee still applies, calculated on the season's listed price, and the platform bills the organization's connected account for it. Source: [requirements, Payments](product/requirements.md#free-seasons-and-offline-payments).
- **Organization:** the body that leagues belong to. Any signed-in user may create one and becomes its first org admin. It has org admins and league organizers, can have multiple locations, connects its own Stripe account, and is the access boundary for its staff. Visitors view its leagues on public pages. Source: [requirements](product/requirements.md#organizations-and-public-registration), [Organizations and staff](product/requirements.md#organizations-and-staff).
- **Organizer:** an org admin, or a league organizer for the leagues assigned to them. Organizers set up seasons, teams, schedules, and payments, place free agents, and confirm or correct scores. Source: [requirements, Organizations and staff](product/requirements.md#organizations-and-staff).
- **Password reset:** letting a user set a new password through a link or code sent by email. Required before the first production release. Source: [requirements, Constraints](product/requirements.md#constraints), [ADR 0011](decisions/0011-transactional-email.md).
- **Payment:** money a player or captain pays an organization for a season, through Stripe Connect into the organization's connected account, with the platform's application fee taken from it. Source: [requirements, Payments](product/requirements.md#payments), [Stripe conventions](conventions/stripe.md).
- **Per-player floor:** the minimum application fee for each roster entry or prepaid slot a payment pays for; it is also the fee billed for each entry in a free season. Its amount is open for the owner. Source: [requirements, Payments](product/requirements.md#payments), [ADR 0012](decisions/0012-stripe-connect.md).
- **Platform bill:** the monthly Stripe invoice the platform sends an organization's connected account for application fees that no payment carried (free seasons, offline payments, some late additions, and the part of a fee too large for its charge). An unpaid bill past its due date puts the organization in `past_due`. Source: [ADR 0012](decisions/0012-stripe-connect.md).
- **Player:** a person on a team's roster. An adult player is always a user with an account; a minor player is a roster entry under a guardian. Source: [requirements, Accounts](product/requirements.md#accounts-players-and-guardians).
- **Player Pro:** a planned paid subscription for players to track their personal stats across leagues. Out of scope; it is why results keep per-player stat lines. Source: [requirements, Future](product/requirements.md#future-out-of-scope).
- **Prepaid slot:** a roster place a team payment paid the per-player floor for before a player filled it, because the payment covers the larger of the roster size and the minimum roster size. A player who accepts an invite later fills it without paying the fee again. Source: [requirements, Payments](product/requirements.md#payments), [ADR 0012](decisions/0012-stripe-connect.md).
- **Publish:** an organizer's action that takes a league or season out of draft and puts it on public pages. Source: [requirements, Organizations and public registration](product/requirements.md#organizations-and-public-registration).
- **Public page:** a page anyone can view without signing in: the organization, location, league, and league registration pages. Public pages are indexed by search engines and show link previews when shared. Source: [requirements](product/requirements.md#organizations-and-public-registration), [ADR 0006](decisions/0006-angular-app-baseline.md).
- **Public layout:** the frame of every page outside `/app/**` (`src/app/public-layout/`): a skip link, a light top bar ("Omni League", then Sign in or the account link), the offline note under the bar, `<main>`, and a footer, with no sidebar. Style A of `design/mockups/300-public-site-navigation.html` and `300-public-site-organization.html`. The app shell stays for `/app/**`.
- **Public list page:** one numbered page of a list on a public page: 5 rows sorted by name ignoring case (a league's seasons: newest start date first), with the list's total. A page past the last, or not a whole number, shows page 1, and a list reaches its first 200 rows (40 pages). Source: `convex/organizations.ts` (`PUBLIC_LIST_PAGE_SIZE`, `PUBLIC_LIST_MAX_PAGE`), #100; `convex/seasons.ts` (`listPublic`) and the page control (`src/app/organizations/page-control.ts`), #127; the organization page's links are #103.
- **Public league page:** the public page of a published league, `/organizations/<org>/leagues/<league>`: its sport and locations, and its published seasons by numbered page (`?page=N#seasons`), each linking to its season page. A draft league has none. Source: `convex/leagues.ts` (`getPublicPage`), `convex/seasons.ts` (`listPublic`), #127.
- **Push notification:** a message the platform sends to a user's device through the browser's push service, shown by the device even when the app is closed: game reminders, schedule changes, and score updates. Source: [requirements, Installable app (PWA)](product/requirements.md#installable-app-pwa).
- **Push subscription:** one browser's permission and address for receiving push notifications, created when a signed-in user turns notifications on for that device. A user can have one per device; it ends when the user turns notifications off, signs out on that device, or the push service reports it expired. Source: [requirements, Installable app (PWA)](product/requirements.md#installable-app-pwa), #83.
- **PWA (progressive web app):** a web app that can be installed, works offline or on a weak signal, and can receive push notifications. Omni League is one, so no separate mobile app is built (owner, 2026-10-09). Source: [requirements, Installable app (PWA)](product/requirements.md#installable-app-pwa).
- **Registration (register):** taking part in a season in one of three ways: a captain registers a whole team, a free agent registers alone, or a person joins an existing team. Each way needs an account. Source: [requirements, Registration](product/requirements.md#registration).
- **Registration deadline:** the date an organizer sets on a season after which teams and free agents can no longer register; registration also closes when the season starts. Source: [requirements, Seasons](product/requirements.md#seasons).
- **Reserved slug:** a slug that no organization, league, or season can take because a route or the demo data uses it, such as `new` (`/app/organizations/new`). It is refused with the slug format message. Source: #64, #99, #125.
- **Return-to URL (`returnTo`):** the query parameter on `/sign-in` and `/sign-up` naming where the browser goes after success. Only a same-origin path is accepted; anything else becomes `/`. Source: [ADR 0008, Return-to-registration URL](decisions/0008-auth-provider.md#return-to-registration-url).
- **Roster:** the list of roster entries on a team for one season. The teams in a season can see each other's rosters. Source: [requirements, Seasons](product/requirements.md#seasons).
- **Roster entry:** one player on a roster: an adult user who accepted an invite or registered, or a minor managed by a guardian. A pending invite is not a roster entry. The per-player floor of the application fee applies to every roster entry. Source: [requirements, Accounts](product/requirements.md#accounts-players-and-guardians), [ADR 0008](decisions/0008-auth-provider.md).
- **Saved copy:** the last result of a deliberately public query that the app keeps on the device, so a recently viewed public page (schedules, standings) shows its last content offline, marked with the time it was saved. Signed-in data is never saved. Source: [ADR 0013](decisions/0013-pwa-stack.md), #82.
- **Saved-copy note:** the line under a public page's heading, shown while the page shows a saved copy and the app is offline: "Saved copy from <time>. It may have changed since." Approved in `design/mockups/82-offline-pages.html` (#82, PR #122).
- **Schedule:** the games of a season, built and published by organizers; a published schedule is deliberately public data. Source: [requirements, Schedule, scores, and standings](product/requirements.md#schedule-scores-and-standings), [Convex conventions](conventions/convex.md#functions).
- **Score report:** a captain's report of a game's score, which an organizer can confirm or correct. Source: [requirements, Schedule, scores, and standings](product/requirements.md#schedule-scores-and-standings).
- **Season:** one run of a league. Registration, teams, the schedule, standings, and fees belong to a season. It is a draft until an organizer publishes it. Source: [requirements, Seasons](product/requirements.md#seasons).
- **Season management page:** the signed-in page at `/app/organizations/<org>/leagues/<league>/seasons/<season>` where organizers see a season's teams against the minimum roster size and its waiting free agents, and place free agents on teams. Proposed in `design/mockups/134-135-season-teams.html` (#134, #135).
- **Season page:** the public page of a published season of a published league, `/organizations/<org>/leagues/<league>/seasons/<season>`: its dates (as stored, no time-zone conversion), format, minimum roster size, and the league's locations. A draft season, or a season of a draft league, has none. Registration actions are added by #129, #133, and #143. Source: `convex/seasons.ts` (`getPublicPage`), #128.
- **Sign-in:** authenticating with email + password or Google, the two methods at launch. Email one-time codes are dropped for now. Source: [requirements, Constraints](product/requirements.md#constraints), [ADR 0008](decisions/0008-auth-provider.md).
- **Sign-in throttle:** the per-address limit on failed password sign-ins: 5 free failures, then a wait of 1 minute doubling to 1 hour, and a hard limit at 100. Each attempt is counted when it starts, and a successful sign-in clears the count. Unknown addresses get identical answers. Source: [ADR 0008, Rate limits and lockout](decisions/0008-auth-provider.md#rate-limits-and-lockout), #63.
- **Sign-up:** creating a password account at `/sign-up` with a name, an email, a password of 15 to 128 characters, and the adult confirmation. Google sign-in creates its account on first use instead. Source: [ADR 0008](decisions/0008-auth-provider.md), #62.
- **Staff:** the org admins and league organizers of an organization. Source: [requirements, Organizations and staff](product/requirements.md#organizations-and-staff).
- **Slug:** the short, URL-safe name of an organization (unique) or a league (unique within its organization), made of lowercase letters and digits joined by single hyphens, 3 to 60 characters; it names the public page, as in `/organizations/<slug>`. Source: `convex/lib/slugs.ts`, #60.
- **Sport:** the game a league plays. Beach volleyball, soccer, and softball at launch, with more to be added. Source: [requirements, Sports](product/requirements.md#sports-initial).
- **Sport view:** the public page of one sport within an organization, `/organizations/<slug>/sports/<segment>` (the kebab-case segment of `convex/lib/sports.ts`: `beach-volleyball`, `soccer`, `softball`), listing that sport's leagues 5 per page (owner, 2026-10-09); the organization sidebar lists sports instead of every league. Proposed in `design/mockups/60-organization-page.html` revision 4 (#100).
- **Sport chips:** **Proposal, not yet chosen by the owner.** The row of links on the public organization page (All sports, then each sport with its league count) that filters the page by sport; each chip opens the sport view's URL. Would replace the sidebar's sports list. Proposed in `design/mockups/300-public-site-organization.html`.
- **Sport location row (`sportLocations`):** one row per organization, sport, and location, holding how many leagues of that sport are held at that location (#101). The rows of a sport are its distinct locations, so `sportLocationCounts[sport]` stays correct when a league later leaves a location. Kept by the tenant writers.
- **Sport-specific rules (sport configuration):** the rules that differ by sport, such as scoring, team size, minimum roster size, and standings, configurable per sport so new sports need no restructuring. Configured in `convex/lib/sports.ts`. Source: [requirements, Sports](product/requirements.md#sports-initial).
- **Standings:** the ranking of teams in a season, calculated by the sport's standings rules. The per-sport defaults are a proposal for the owner to confirm. Source: [requirements, Schedule, scores, and standings](product/requirements.md#schedule-scores-and-standings).
- **Stat line:** one player's recorded statistics for one game. Results keep stat lines per player, not only team scores. Source: [requirements, Schedule, scores, and standings](product/requirements.md#schedule-scores-and-standings).
- **Team:** a group of players in one season, with a captain; it is confirmed once its roster meets the minimum roster size and its fees are paid. A person is on at most one team in a season. Source: [requirements, Registration](product/requirements.md#registration), [Payments](product/requirements.md#payments).
- **Undo window:** the 10 minutes after an org admin revokes a staff invite in which Undo on the Staff screen sets it back to pending, with the same link and expiry. The server enforces it (`invites.restore`); a refused Undo says why. Source: [#211](https://github.com/jasonbryan/omni-league/issues/211), `design/mockups/67-organization-staff.html` (decision 7).
- **User:** a person with an account. Every adult player and every organizer is a user; in the backend the app's `users` record is keyed on the auth provider's user id. Source: [requirements, Accounts](product/requirements.md#accounts-players-and-guardians), [ADR 0008](decisions/0008-auth-provider.md).
- **Visitor:** anyone viewing a public page. A visitor goes from a public registration page to sign-in and back. Source: [requirements](product/requirements.md#organizations-and-public-registration), [ADR 0008](decisions/0008-auth-provider.md).
- **Waitlist:** the queue of teams that registered after a season was full. A waitlisted team is not yet a team and has no roster entries; an organizer promotes it, which creates the team. Source: [requirements, Seasons](product/requirements.md#seasons).

## Process terms

- **Acceptance criteria:** the checkable Given/When/Then statements on an issue. Each one must be provable by a test or a screenshot. See [dev cycle, Plan](process/dev-cycle.md#1-plan) and [feature template](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/feature.yml).
- **Accepted technology:** a framework, package, service, or tool named in an Accepted ADR on `main`. Anything else needs `gate:technology`; minor and patch bumps of accepted packages do not. See [AGENTS.md, Approval gates](../AGENTS.md#approval-gates).
- **ADR (architecture decision record):** a numbered file in `docs/decisions/`, written from [the template](decisions/0000-template.md), that records a `gate:technology` decision with at least two options. See [dev cycle, Gates](process/dev-cycle.md#3-gates).
  - **Accepted:** the status the planner writes in the gate PR. It takes effect when the owner merges the PR.
  - **Proposed:** not a status written in the file. A decision is a proposal while its PR is open and unmerged; a rejected proposal is closed unmerged and never reaches `main`.
  - **Superseded by NNNN:** the status edited onto an Accepted ADR on `main` when a later ADR replaces it.
- **Affected e2e specs:** the e2e spec files a PR's `ci` runs: the ones its changed files map to in `scripts/e2e-affected.mjs`, plus the smoke and harness specs. A shared or unmapped change, a release PR, and any failure to detect run the full suite instead. See [CI, Affected e2e specs and the nightly run](process/ci.md#affected-e2e-specs-and-the-nightly-run).
- **Anonymous local Convex backend:** the Convex backend run on the developer's machine with `CONVEX_AGENT_MODE=anonymous npx convex dev`, with no Convex account; used for development and e2e. The e2e run starts its own, the **local e2e backend**, through `scripts/e2e-convex.mjs` from `.convex-e2e/`, pinned in `e2e/support/convex-backend.json`. See [README](../README.md#run-the-convex-backend), [README, E2E tests](../README.md#e2e-tests), and [ADR 0005](decisions/0005-e2e-and-accessibility-testing.md).
- **App shell:** the dashboard-style layout of the signed-in pages under `/app/**` only (the public pages use the public layout): a skip link, a top bar (app name, then "Sign in" or, when signed in, the account link), a sidebar when the page has an organization (its overview, its sports with league counts, and its locations, as proposed in revision 4 of `design/mockups/60-organization-page.html`; a sheet behind a menu button below 1024px), and `<main>` with content in panels. - **Target check:** `assertLocalTarget` and `resolveTarget` in `scripts/e2e-convex.mjs`: before any `convex env set` or `convex run`, they refuse every target other than the local e2e backend on `http://127.0.0.1:3210`. See [README, E2E tests](../README.md#e2e-tests).
- **Tenant (tenancy):** the organization is the access boundary for its staff (owner answer, 2026-10-09). In tests, a tenant is a seeded organization with its leagues, teams, and users, and each e2e test seeds its own and asserts only on its data. See [requirements, Organizations and staff](product/requirements.md#organizations-and-staff) and [ADR 0005](decisions/0005-e2e-and-accessibility-testing.md).
- **Tenant writers:** `convex/lib/tenantWrites.ts`, the only code that inserts, updates, or deletes organizations, locations, leagues, and `sportLocations` rows. In the same mutation as the row, it keeps each row's `sortName` (the name lowercased, for the case-insensitive index order), the organization's `leagueCount` and `locationCount`, which the public page shows as totals (#100), and its per-sport `sportLeagueCounts` and `sportLocationCounts` with the `sportLocations` rows behind them (#101).
- **Test-only function:** an internal function in `convex/testing.ts` that only the e2e run calls (`testing:reset`, `testing:ping`, and the tenant seeds `testing:seedLeague` and `testing:addLeague`); each starts with the `IS_TEST` guard in `convex/lib/testOnly.ts`. See [README, E2E tests](../README.md#e2e-tests) and [ADR 0005](decisions/0005-e2e-and-accessibility-testing.md).
- **Test plan:** the issue section naming the unit, Convex, and e2e tests that prove each acceptance criterion. See [dev cycle, Plan](process/dev-cycle.md#1-plan).
- **Token sheet:** the page in `design/mockups/` that records proposed design token values, base styles, and their contrast results; the issue that applies them copies the values into `src/styles/tokens.css`. The first is `design/mockups/61-token-sheet.html`. See [ADR 0007](decisions/0007-ui-library-and-styling.md).
- **Unit tests:** Angular component and service tests run with Vitest through `ng test` (`npm test`). See [ADR 0004](decisions/0004-unit-and-convex-test-runners.md).
- **UX checklist:** the list at the end of [UX conventions](conventions/ux.md#ux-checklist) that the implementer ticks in the PR body and the tester and PR reviewer check, for what a PR adds or changes on a screen or task flow. Separate from the **Manual WCAG checklist**.
- **VAPID keys:** the key pair that identifies the platform to browsers' push services when it sends push notifications (RFC 8292). The private key is a secret per deployment; production's is owner-held. Source: [ADR 0013](decisions/0013-pwa-stack.md).
- **Viewport-agnostic test:** an e2e test whose result does not depend on the screen size. It passes `VIEWPORT_AGNOSTIC` from `e2e/support/viewport-agnostic.ts` and runs in `chromium-desktop` (and `webkit-mobile` on a WebKit run) but not in `chromium-mobile`, to save CI minutes. A test that checks layout, accessibility, screenshots, focus, or responsive behaviour is never viewport-agnostic. See [README, E2E tests](../README.md#e2e-tests).
- **`warm-cache`:** the `CI` job that runs only on `main` pushes changing `package-lock.json` or `ci.yml`, filling the npm cache that PR runs restore. It is not a required check. See [CI, What runs when](process/ci.md#what-runs-when).
- **Web app manifest:** the JSON file (`manifest.webmanifest`) linked from every page that gives the app's name, icons, colours, start page, and standalone display, which browsers read to install it. Source: [requirements, Installable app (PWA)](product/requirements.md#installable-app-pwa), #80.
- **Worktree:** an isolated git checkout per agent and branch, so parallel work does not collide; branches are named `<type>/<issue>-<slug>`. See [dev cycle, Implement](process/dev-cycle.md#4-implement).
